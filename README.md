Stitch is a platform built by [RMI](https://rmi.org) to integrate diverse sector-specific datasets and enhance them with AI, starting with oil & gas. It consolidates fragmented upstream datasets into a single, provenance-aware view of oil & gas fields, backed by a database and exposed through a UI and API. It's built for climate researchers, energy analysts, and policy teams who need trustworthy, source-attributed data.
